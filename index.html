Assembly & Safety

  1. Unpack the cajon and lay out all components on a soft surface.
  2. Unfold the panels until they click into the primary hinge positions.
  3. Install and tighten all supplied fixings and fasteners using the included tools.
  4. Adjust snare springs to desired tension for your preferred tone.
  5. Place the cajon on a stable, level surface before playing.
Warning: Do not sit on the cajon unless it is fully assembled and all fixings are securely fastened and tightened.
