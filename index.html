CRITICAL SAFETY WARNING

Never sit on the Camino cajon until assembly is fully complete. Ensure all hand-screws and locking pins are fully engaged, aligned, and tightly locked before applying any body weight. Sitting on a partially assembled cajon will damage the hinges and may cause personal injury.
